### IF: BLOG POST / DEV DIARY

Write a complete blog post.

**Structure:**
- **Title**: Engaging, SEO-aware (include INWIGO + key topic term)
- **Meta description**: 150-160 characters for search/social preview
- **Introduction**: Hook the reader — a story, a surprising fact, or a compelling question
- **3-5 body sections** with H2 headers
- **Conclusion**: Genuine, personal close — not a canned summary
- **CTA**: Link to Discord, wishlist, or specific action relevant to the post

Write with the INWIGO voice: warm, quirky, honest. Dev diaries in particular should feel like a real person wrote them at midnight after a good build.
