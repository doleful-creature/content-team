## Brand Overview

**INWIGO** is a multiplayer VR horror-survival game set in a Halloween carnival rendered in a hand-drawn art style inspired by Don't Starve.

- **Genre anchor**: Co-op chaos meets lighthearted horror — think Lethal Company's emergent social disasters, but you're actually *inside* a haunted carnival.
- **Art anchor**: Every corner looks hand-crafted. Don't Starve's spindly, expressive world — but in VR, which means you're *in* it.
- **Emotional promise**: "You'll scream. You'll laugh. You'll immediately call your friends."

## Brand Voice

### The Four Tone Pillars

1. **Playfully Spooky** — Scary-adjacent, never grimdark. Think haunted house that winks at you. The scares are real but they end in laughter, not nightmares.

2. **Community-First** — The best INWIGO moments happen with other people. Copy always celebrates the social, shared experience. We talk to groups, not individuals in isolation.

3. **Chaotically Charming** — Embrace the weird, the unexpected, the hilarious failures alongside genuine scares. The chaos *is* the feature. Lean into it.

4. **Authentically Indie** — We're not a AAA publisher. That's a strength. Genuine enthusiasm, behind-the-scenes moments, real developer voice > polished corporate PR speak.

---

### Personality Traits

- Enthusiastic without being breathless
- Self-aware and slightly irreverent
- Warm and inclusive — not a gatekeeper
- Slightly mysterious (always hint there's more to discover)
- Celebrates player creativity (screenshot their chaos, quote their reactions)

---

### Voice DO's

- Use gaming vernacular naturally (don't force it)
- Reference carnival imagery: big top, funhouse mirrors, ferris wheels, cotton candy, clowns, carnival prizes, game booths, fog machines
- Reference Halloween imagery: pumpkins, jack-o-lanterns, ghosts, spiders, cobwebs, candy, costumes, trick-or-treat
- Use second person ("you", "your friends") to pull readers in
- Short, punchy sentences win
- Celebrate community moments (reactions, clips, memes)
- Be honest about indie development

### Voice DON'Ts

- No grimdark or genuinely disturbing imagery — always lighthearted
- No overselling: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "best-in-class"
- No corporate PR speak: "paradigm", "ecosystem", "leverage"
- Don't punch at competitors — let the comps speak for positioning only
- Avoid gendered default language

## Platform-Specific Voice Guide

### Twitter/X
- 1-2 punchy sentences maximum for the main post text
- Hashtags as a separate line (not embedded in tweet body)
- Jokes, reactions, and memes are on-brand and encouraged
- Reply to tags/mentions with personality — don't be a bot
- Threads for announcements that need more than 280 chars

### Instagram
- Captions can run 2-4 paragraphs — visual does heavy lifting, caption adds story
- Lead line must be compelling (it's the hook before "more")
- Use line breaks generously for readability
- End with a clear CTA
- Hashtag block goes at the very end (30 hashtags max)

### TikTok
- The hook is the product — if the first 2 seconds don't stop scroll, nothing else matters
- Lean into: jump scares, chaos moments, funny fails, behind-the-scenes dev content
- Text overlays should echo or contrast the visual moment
- Music choice dramatically affects performance — describe the energy needed
- Shorter > longer. 15-30 seconds usually outperforms 60+

### Discord
- Use Discord markdown: `## Heading`, `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `> blockquote`
- Emojis: yes, but tasteful — 2-4 per announcement, not every sentence
- Structure with clear sections so people can skim
- Warm, community-forward tone — these are your people, treat them like it
- Always include a CTA or conversation prompt

### Reddit (r/VRGaming, r/gaming, r/indiegaming, r/gamedev)
- More earnest and informative — Reddit sees through marketing
- Always be upfront that you're the developer
- Show don't tell — lead with gameplay footage or screenshots
- Engage with comments genuinely; don't just drop and disappear
- Share things of actual value to the community, not just promotion

### Press / Influencer (Email)
- Professional, but let genuine enthusiasm show through
- Lead with what makes INWIGO interesting **to them specifically**
- Clear, direct ask — don't bury it
- Under 200 words for cold outreach
- Easy access to everything they need

## Visual Identity (Content Direction Notes)

- **Art style descriptor**: Hand-drawn, slightly rough-edged linework. Characters are tall and spindly with expressive faces. Palette is desaturated with selective saturated pops — glowing carnival lights, pumpkin orange, ghost white.
- **Environment motifs**: Big top tents, ferris wheels, funhouse mirrors, carousel horses, game booths, fog rolling across the ground, jack-o-lanterns on every surface.
- **Screenshot guidance**: Favor atmospheric wide shots showing the carnival + player characters. Capture chaos moments with multiple players. Glowing lighting is your friend.
- **Clip guidance**: Capture the exact moment of a scare + reaction. Funny fails are gold. Show the "oh no" to "everyone screaming" arc.
