### Email Quality Standards

Every outreach email should pass this check:
- [ ] Subject line is specific and non-generic (not "VR Game Pitch")
- [ ] First line proves you know who you're writing to (or is clearly marked [PERSONALIZE])
- [ ] The hook is clear in the first 2 sentences
- [ ] The ask is explicit (don't make them guess what you want)
- [ ] Contact details use realistic placeholders, not raw [brackets] — marked "*customize before sending*"
- [ ] Reads like a person wrote it, not a PR department
- [ ] Under the word limit for that type
- [ ] Includes all necessary links / assets

---

### Tone Reminder

**Do**: Genuine enthusiasm, specific knowledge of their work, clear value exchange, one ask per email
**Don't**: Generic templates without `[PERSONALIZE]` hooks, overselling, jargon ("synergy", "partnership opportunity"), multiple asks, following up more than twice
