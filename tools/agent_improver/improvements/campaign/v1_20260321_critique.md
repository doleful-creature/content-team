### Issues

**[HIGH] Output length not managed — calendar will be truncated**
- Problem: This skill asks for a 10-section document including a full multi-week content calendar. For 6+ week campaigns, Claude frequently truncates the output before completing the calendar, leaving the most practical section incomplete.
- Impact: The most actionable deliverable (the calendar) is the one most likely to be cut off.
- Fix: Add explicit instructions: "If the campaign is 6+ weeks, you may abbreviate the calendar from Week 4 onward with a pattern note (e.g., 'Weeks 4-6 follow the same cadence as Week 3 — [pattern]. Specific topics available on request.'). Completeness matters more than exhaustiveness."

**[HIGH] Content calendar template is too sparse**
- Problem: The calendar table shows a header row and 4 sample rows (Weeks 1-2) followed by "Continue for all weeks." Claude interprets "continue" minimally, often producing generic topic labels like "TikTok content" or "Instagram post."
- Impact: The campaign plan's most practical section contains vague placeholders instead of actionable specifics.
- Fix: Explicitly require: (1) minimum 4 rows per week, (2) real topic names ("Behind the scenes: funhouse mirror art" not "Dev content"), (3) Claude should draw on the creative angles defined in Section 2 when populating the calendar.

**[MEDIUM] KPI baseline has no guidance**
- Problem: The metrics table asks for "Current Baseline" but the marketer at near-launch stage may not have established numbers. Claude will either leave the field blank or invent numbers.
- Impact: KPIs without baselines are untrackable; invented baselines give false confidence.
- Fix: Instruct Claude: "If baseline is unknown, write 'TBD — measure before campaign start' and provide an estimated range based on typical indie VR game pre-launch metrics (e.g., Discord: 200-500, wishlist: 500-2,000)."

**[MEDIUM] Influencer hit list is always the same template**
- Problem: The hit list shows `[VR creator, 100k+]`, `[Horror gaming creator]`, etc. as fixed placeholders regardless of campaign type. A community-building campaign needs different creator targets than a launch campaign.
- Impact: Every campaign plan produces the same generic influencer categories.
- Fix: Instruct Claude to make the hit list campaign-type-specific. For launch: YouTube reviewers + Twitch streamers. For Halloween event: short-form horror creators. For community-building: Discord/Reddit community builders + VR-focused micro-influencers.

**[LOW] Campaign hashtag guidance is minimal**
- Problem: "Campaign hashtag: #INWIGO + campaign-specific tag" gives no guidance on what makes a good campaign hashtag (short, unique, searchable, not already used).
- Impact: Minor — hashtag quality may vary.
- Fix: Add one-liner: "Campaign hashtag should be: under 20 chars, not already in use, memorable standalone (e.g., #INWIGOLaunch, #CarnivalOpens, #INWIGOHaunted)."

**[LOW] Weekly checklist is the same regardless of campaign phase**
- Problem: The weekly execution checklist is a one-size-fits-all template. Week 1 of a pre-launch campaign is very different from Week 5 of a post-launch campaign.
- Impact: Minor — the checklist is still useful, just not phase-aware.
- Fix: Note that the checklist can be customized per phase; include one phase-specific variation as an example.

- Asset production checklist with "must have vs. nice to have" distinction is immediately actionable
