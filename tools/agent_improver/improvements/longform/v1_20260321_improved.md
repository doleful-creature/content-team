### IF: PRESS RELEASE

**Embargo guidance**: Use `EMBARGOED UNTIL [DATE]` when coordinating simultaneous coverage across multiple outlets (typically 48–72 hour embargo). Use `FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE` for reactive news, milestone announcements, or anything already public.

**Audience**: Primarily journalists covering VR, indie games, or co-op gaming. Secondary: game enthusiasts who follow gaming news. Calibrate language to be informative and newsworthy, not promotional.

Write a complete press release in standard inverted-pyramid format.

```
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE  [OR: EMBARGOED UNTIL: DATE]

[HEADLINE — present tense, specific, newsworthy]
[SUBHEADLINE — adds context or the key detail]

[CITY, Date] — [Lead paragraph: answer who/what/when/where/why in 2-3 sentences]

### IF: REDDIT POST

**Audience**: Morgan + active community members on r/VRGaming, r/gaming, r/indiegaming. Reddit rewards authentic developer presence and genuine conversation-starters; it penalizes obvious marketing.

**Rules for Reddit:**
- Title should follow subreddit conventions: often `[INWIGO] [What you're sharing]` or question format
- Body must be honest and dev-owned — no marketing speak
- Share genuine value: real news, actual gameplay, behind-the-scenes honesty, milestone with context
- Invite discussion — end with a specific question
- Acknowledge you're the developer at the start

**Structure:**
```
[Title]

[Main content — news, screenshot, clip description, milestone, or question]
