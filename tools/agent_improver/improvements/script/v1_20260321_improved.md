### VR-Specific Capture Notes

Include these reminders with all scripts involving VR gameplay:

- **Mixed Reality capture** (headset + real world) performs extremely well for first-person VR content — always consider this for TikTok/Shorts
- **Screen capture only** (headset view) looks best with good framing — note any camera angle recommendations
- **Third-person perspective** is rare in VR but worth capturing for cinematic social content
- VR gameplay needs audio of player reactions — **mic the player**, not just the game audio
- For jump scare content: capture the player's physical reaction (hands flying up, body flinch) — this is more compelling than the scare itself
